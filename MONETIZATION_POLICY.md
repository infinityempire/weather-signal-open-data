# Monetization and Disclosure Policy

Weather Signal works without monetization. The application has no advertising script, affiliate links, paywall, click incentive, or revenue guarantee.

## Optional support and sponsor links

The site can display `PAYPAL_ME_LINK`, `SPONSOR_URL`, and `SPONSOR_LABEL` only through GitHub Actions secrets. Any link is visibly placed in a dedicated **Support** block, carries `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`, and states that it does not affect forecasts or signal labels.

Never describe a sponsor or support link as being endorsed by NOAA or the National Weather Service. Do not add a sponsor link that resembles an alert, forecast control, weather source, or safety instruction.

## Data and product boundaries

NWS states that its information is public domain unless specifically noted otherwise and can be used for lawful purposes, but it forbids implying NOAA/NWS endorsement or presenting modified data as official government material.[1] This product must preserve source links, identify its own transformations as unofficial, and direct visitors to weather.gov for official information.

## Required review before activation

Before setting any optional monetization secret, the owner must verify the destination, obtain approval from any relevant network, provide required disclosures, and ensure local legal compliance. Activation does not guarantee traffic, conversions, payment, advertising approval, or revenue.
