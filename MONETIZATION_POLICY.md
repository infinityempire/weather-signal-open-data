# Monetization and Disclosure Policy

Dependency Stability Lens works without monetization. The application has no advertising script, affiliate links, paywall, click incentive, or revenue guarantee.

## Optional support and sponsor links

The site can display `PAYPAL_ME_LINK`, `SPONSOR_URL`, and `SPONSOR_LABEL` only through secure environment variables. Any link is visibly placed in a dedicated **Optional support** block, carries `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"`, and states that it does not affect scores, source selection or incident interpretation.

Never describe a sponsor or support link as being endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic, GitHub or any tracked provider. Do not add a sponsor link that resembles an official incident, provider status banner, score control or source citation.

## Data and product boundaries

This product must preserve official source links and identify all scoring, normalization and portfolio posture labels as independent transformations. It must not imply provider endorsement, describe its score as an SLA or official uptime record, or obscure the original status source. The site directs visitors to each provider’s official status page for operational decisions.[1] [2] [3]

## Required review before activation

Before setting any optional monetization secret, the owner must verify the destination, obtain approval from any relevant network, provide required disclosures, and ensure local legal compliance. Activation does not guarantee traffic, conversions, payment, advertising approval, or revenue.
